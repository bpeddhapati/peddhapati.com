about me.

Personal

Married with two kids and living in the Chicago suburbs, I enjoy traveling with my family, exploring nature, and capturing life's memorable moments through photography. Building and transforming things has always been a passion of mine, from creating technology platforms and leading high-performing teams to turning unfinished spaces into welcoming living areas and immersive home theater experiences. A longtime BMW enthusiast, I have a deep appreciation for great engineering and craftsmanship. Outside of work, I value time with family, staying active, and enjoying immersive movie and music experiences in our home theater.

Professional

I build technology platforms and the teams that bring them to life, with a focus on data, AI, and innovation at scale. As Chief Technology Officer at Envestnet, I lead technology, engineering, and information security for a leading wealth management platform. Prior to Envestnet, I led mortgage technology at CoreLogic and held senior leadership roles across Nielsen businesses. For a more detailed overview of my experience and background, please visit my LinkedIn profile.
